# Third-Party Notices

The executable JAR bundles third-party runtime libraries. Their coordinates,
versions, copyright attributions, license summaries, and redistribution terms
are recorded in [`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`](src/main/resources/META-INF/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt).
The same notice file is included in the executable JAR and distribution ZIP.

The project license is Apache-2.0; see [LICENSE](LICENSE). The runtime JAR
includes it at `META-INF/LICENSE` and includes jsoup's license at
`META-INF/jsoup/LICENSE`.
